# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 21, 2026
**Publisher:** Fatih Özbay ("FoGuTech", "we", "us", or "our")

This Privacy Policy applies to every mobile game published by FoGuTech on the Apple App Store, including **LightLine** and any future titles, unless an individual game publishes a superseding notice in-app. Throughout this document, "the App" refers to whichever FoGuTech game you are using.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

Our games do not require an account. We do not operate a server that collects or stores your personal information. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.

## 2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

Our games typically store gameplay data locally on your device using Apple's `UserDefaults` so that your progress survives between sessions. The exact contents depend on the game, but generally include:

- Game progress, scores, and statistics (e.g., best scores, games played, streaks).
- Preferences (e.g., theme, sound, haptics, selected language).
- Flags used by the game (e.g., whether first-launch onboarding has been shown).

This data never leaves your device through us, and you can delete all of it at any time by deleting the App.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our games display advertisements (which may include banner, interstitial, and rewarded formats) provided by **Google AdMob**. To serve ads, the AdMob SDK may process information such as:

- Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), if you grant permission via the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.
- Technical data such as device model, operating system version, IP address, and approximate region.
- Standard ad-serving signals such as whether an ad was shown, tapped, or completed.

If you deny tracking in the ATT prompt, or opt out via iOS Settings, AdMob will serve non-personalized ads instead. You will still see ads, but they will not be tailored using your advertising identifier.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own policies:

- Google Privacy & Terms: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

We do not combine any AdMob-provided data with data we collect, because we do not collect data ourselves.

## 4. App Tracking Transparency

Because our games include AdMob, iOS will prompt you to allow or deny tracking the first time the App runs. Your choice is respected and can be changed at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** or **Settings → [App name]**.

## 5. In-App Purchases

Unless a specific game's in-app disclosure states otherwise, our games are free to play and do not currently offer in-app purchases. If a future game introduces in-app purchases, payments are processed by Apple — we do not receive or store your payment information.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our games are suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## 7. Data Security

Because game data is stored only on your device, its security is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, full-disk encryption). We do not operate a server that holds your information.

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Opt out of personalized advertising via the iOS ATT setting.
- Delete all locally stored data by deleting the App.
- Contact us with any privacy question (see below).

Residents of the EU/UK, California, or other jurisdictions with specific privacy laws: because we do not collect personal information ourselves, most data-subject rights under laws such as GDPR or CCPA do not apply to us. For requests concerning data processed by AdMob, please contact Google directly using the links in Section 3.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Effective date" at the top of this document will be updated. Material changes will be highlighted in the relevant App's release notes when possible.
